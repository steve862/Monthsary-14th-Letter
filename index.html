Happy 14th Monthsary!

Happy 14th monthsary, dear Adi! Today is a special day I treasure a lot. I remember the day we just met, and I’ve got my eyes on you. Eventually, we just have our first conversation that turns into emotional intimacy and makes me feel I’m being valued more than enough. On that day, I’ve got my mind on you.

Many things have changed since distance became our problem. Frequent misunderstandings and arguments, even in simple things, test our resilience, but despite that, even almost breaking us apart, you keep fixing things to make our relationship healthier, and I am thankful for you choosing me over and over again with your best.

There are some of the nights I can’t sleep where I feel alone and sad reminiscing about our past when distance and time are not much of a problem. I seek love, attention, and time, hoping you were here with me. It hurts me a lot, and I experience frequent overthinking, but I keep holding on, waiting for the day when distance and time won’t be a big deal between us.

Meeting you was never an accident, and choosing you was never a mistake. You’re a wonderful experience, and the best of all I had, which I was proud of. Every teardrop was never a waste, and loving you was never a sacrifice, for it is devotion. I never hesitate to love you.

Loving you was never a question, it is a riddle that has indefinite meaning and cannot have a simple purpose of existence. I love you, and I will continue to prove it in every way, not just by words and promises, but more than anything you deserve.

From: Adi
